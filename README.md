# ✈️ Airline Operations & Passenger Analytics

**A Python & Pandas Business Analytics Project**

**Prepared by:** Prof. Pooja Garje | Business Analyst Trainer

## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses Python, Pandas, and NumPy to explore airline passenger data and identify patterns in passenger profiles, flight characteristics, service ratings, satisfaction, and flight delays.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) workflow and shows how data can support business understanding and decision-making in the airline industry.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Inspect dataset structure, size, and data types.
- Identify missing values, duplicate records, and data-quality issues.
- Analyze passenger demographics and customer segments.
- Explore flight distances across travel classes and customer types.
- Compare airline service ratings.
- Measure passenger satisfaction across different groups.
- Analyze departure and arrival delay patterns.
- Develop evidence-based business insights and recommendations.

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. **Data Loading** – Import the dataset and preview records.
2. **Data Understanding** – Examine rows, columns, data types, and summary statistics.
3. **Data Cleaning** – Check missing values, duplicates, and unnecessary columns.
4. **Passenger Segmentation** – Analyze passenger groups by demographics, travel purpose, customer type, and class.
5. **Flight Analysis** – Explore flight-distance patterns.
6. **Service Analysis** – Compare service ratings overall and across travel classes.
7. **Satisfaction Analysis** – Calculate satisfaction rates for different passenger segments.
8. **Delay Analysis** – Examine flight delays and their association with satisfaction.
9. **Business Insights** – Summarize findings and potential business considerations.
